fallback. Published tool names, inputs, outputs, mutations and concurrency contracts are preserved.

No provider or SQL change is introduced.

including their original failure diagnostics and KAN-325 Auth/OAuth/disabled-worker cleanup proof.

receipts and final acceptance. Draft-skipped jobs are not passing evidence. This document describes
a candidate, not an accepted deployment; KAN-327 stays In Review until controller acceptance.
