An optional Google Calendar connection

Sign in with Google to identify your Sabah One (HELM) account. Connecting Google Calendar is a separate choice on the Integrations page.

With your permission, Sabah One (HELM) reads your calendar list and displays your events. You can create, edit and delete events on calendars where you already have permission. Changes are sent to Google Calendar by Sabah One (HELM)’s calendar service.

You can disconnect a Google account in Integrations → Google Calendar. Read the privacy notice for the data used, storage and removal details.
